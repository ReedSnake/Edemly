For teams

Work through conversations without losing the decisions.

Edemly gives teams a single place for group chats, calls, media, notes, and company context.

Download Leave feedback

The product is useful for project groups, student teams, agencies, and small companies that want chat history to stay connected to people, files, and next steps.

Instead of spreading context across separate chats, notes, files, and planning tools, Edemly keeps the everyday communication flow closer to the decisions that come from it.

Future releases can add platform-specific packages while this site keeps one consistent release catalog.
