One calm place for chats, context, and next steps.

Edemly is a desktop messenger that helps communication feel less scattered. Instead of keeping messages in one app, notes in another, and team context somewhere else, Edemly brings the basic workflow into one clean space.

You can chat, create groups, share media, use audio features, switch between companies, customize themes, and keep useful notes connected to people or conversations.
